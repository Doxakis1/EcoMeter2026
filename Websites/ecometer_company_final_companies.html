📘 How EcoMeter Works

1
Describe your project — Type a description of your business idea, investment plan, or project in the input box. No technical knowledge required.
2
AI analysis — EcoMeter reads all relevant EU biodiversity legislation and regulations automatically, and checks whether your project poses any environmental risk.
3
Get your Risk Score — You receive a Biodiversity Risk Score on a scale from 0 to 10, where a lower score means lower environmental risk. The score indicates whether your project falls in the low, medium, or high risk range, along with specific regulatory references and recommendations.
4
Share with your bank — Use the Share with Bank button to send your encrypted assessment with a unique reference number to your bank for financing approval.
💡 A low risk score significantly increases your chances of loan approval and demonstrates EU compliance to investors.
