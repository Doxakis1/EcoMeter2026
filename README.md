EcoMeter 🌿
Biodiversity Risk Intelligence
EcoMeter is an AI-powered tool that gives banks and businesses a Biodiversity Risk Score for any project or investment — based on EU legislation, in minutes instead of weeks.
The Problem
Banks that finance projects violating EU biodiversity laws face a double risk:

They lose the loan if the company gets fined and goes bankrupt
The ECB fines the bank itself for poor risk management

The Solution
EcoMeter analyzes any business idea or investment and instantly returns a Biodiversity Risk Score from 0 to 10 with regulatory references and recommendations.
Who It's For

🏦 Banks — assess investments before approving loans
🏭 Businesses — check compliance before applying for financing
🚀 Entrepreneurs — come to the bank ready with a verified score

How It Works
EcoMeter uses a RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) system built on EU biodiversity legislation, real-time regulatory updates, and scientific data.
